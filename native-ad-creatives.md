## 🎨 Design Guidelines for Native Ads

### Color Palette (Dharmic Brand):
- **Primary**: Saffron (#FF6B35), Deep Orange (#FF4500)
- **Accent**: Gold (#D4AF37), Maroon (#800020)
- **Background**: Cream (#FFF8DC)

### Typography:
- **Headlines**: Poppins Bold (English), Noto Serif Devanagari (Hindi)
- **Body**: Poppins Regular
- **Size**: Minimum 18px for mobile readability

### Native Feel Checklist:
- ✅ Realistic home settings (not studio)
- ✅ Authentic clothing (kurta, casual Indian wear)
- ✅ Natural lighting (avoid studio lighting)
- ✅ Real emotions (not stock photo smiles)
- ✅ Traditional decor elements (religious books, family photos)
- ✅ Subtle branding (Om symbol watermark, no logos)

## 📊 A/B Testing Strategy

### Test Variables:
1. **Language Mix**: Hindi-first vs English-first vs Bilingual
2. **Emotion Type**: Pain (distant son) vs Hope (transformation)
3. **CTA Style**: Direct ("Take Assessment") vs Curiosity ("Discover How")
4. **Social Proof**: Number focus (12,847) vs Percentage (94% success)

### Recommended Test Schedule:
- **Week 1-2**: Test all 4 creatives with same copy
- **Week 3-4**: Winning creative with 3 copy variations
- **Week 5+**: Scale winning combination
